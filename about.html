Background

I'm currently a Computer Engineering student at the University of Pittsburgh. I expect to graduate in December 2026, and I'm excited to start my career. I'm especially interested in working with embedded systems or developing FPGAs.

Throughout my time at Pitt, I've had the opportunity to work with everything from oscilloscopes and multimeters to microcontrollers. I really enjoy working with embedded systems and seeing my code come to life. One of my favorite projects so far has been building a digital camera, which was a really exciting experience. I'm also interested in FPGA development, although I have less experience in that area. One project I found especially interesting was designing my own multi-cycle 32-bit MIPS CPU.

I'm from Pittsburgh, and when I'm not working on projects, I enjoy traveling, journaling, scrapbooking, and photography. I've recently gotten into photography and love the idea of being able to capture memories from my travels. I'm always looking for new places to explore and experiences to document.
